# Privacy

The archive must not publish private donor information, personal addresses, private email addresses, contracts, credentials, or restricted materials.

## Principles

- Collect the minimum contact information needed for submissions and research requests.
- Keep private contact information out of public records and search indexes.
- Store consent, restrictions, and takedown status with the related record.
- Separate internal notes from public notes.
- Support export and deletion workflows for user accounts where applicable.
- Use privacy-aware analytics only after owner approval.

## Public Submissions

Submissions enter a review queue. Files require malware scanning and rights review before processing. No user-provided file becomes public by default.
